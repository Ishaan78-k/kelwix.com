Our Heritage: The Loom & The Legacy

In the narrow, aromatic lanes of Bhuleshwar, where temple bells of Mumbadevi mingle with the whispers of merchants, grandfather Hemchand Agrawal laid the foundation of our saree salon. He believed an authentic handloom saree is an heirloom manuscript woven in silk and real silver zari.

For over three decades our family has nurtured direct bonds with master weaver clusters. Every weave is conceived over visits to loom pits and conversations over cutting chai in our Madhav Bagh showroom.

Today, led by the second generation, we preserve antique nakshas, commission slow kadhwa brocades and present brides with museum-grade textiles for generations.
